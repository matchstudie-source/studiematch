Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Dansk

Who we are

Studiematch (studiematch.dk) is the data controller for the information described here. You can always write to us at matchstudie@gmail.com. The Danish version of this policy is the legally binding one.

Without an account

You can use Studiematch without creating an account. Your profile (degree, filters, saved jobs and what the site has learned about you) is then only stored in your own browser. If you upload a CV without being logged in, it is read in your browser and sent nowhere.

With an account

If you create an account, we store:

  • Your email, your name (if you enter it) and an encrypted version of your password. We cannot see the password itself.
  • Your profile: degrees, semester, filters, skills, interests, previous jobs, saved and hidden jobs, and your language choice.
  • The text of your CV if you upload one, and what we found in it. We don't keep the file itself.
  • When you signed up and when you were last logged in.
  • Where you came to us from: your answer to "Where did you hear about Studiematch?", if you answered, and the page or link you first came from.
  • What you do on the site while logged in: which jobs you open, save, hide and apply for, and which filters you use. This is stored with your account.

We use the information to find jobs that fit you, to let you log in from several devices, and to understand what works on the site so we can improve it. The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you create the account. You can withdraw it by deleting your account.

Job emails

If you have turned on job emails, we send you an email when new jobs fit your profile, at most once a day or once a week, as you choose. We store which jobs we have sent you so you don't get the same ones twice. You can unsubscribe with one click at the bottom of every email or under Profile.

AI

When you are logged in and upload a CV, we may send the text to our AI provider Anthropic, which reads it and finds skills and experience. The same goes for the name of a degree we don't know. We also use AI to translate job descriptions into English. Anthropic does not use data from its API to train its models.

Statistics for visitors

We count how many people visit the site, where they come from and what they click, so we can improve the site and tell employers and job portals how many people we send their way. We don't use cookies for this or third parties like Google Analytics, and we don't store your IP address.

If you are not logged in, you are counted with a code that changes every day. We can see what a visitor did on a given day, but not who it was, and we cannot follow you from day to day. If you are logged in, it is stored with your account, as described above.

If you are logged in, we use one necessary cookie to keep you logged in.

Companies

If you write to us as a company or post a job, we store the company name, your email and what you wrote, so we can reply and show the post.

Who has access

Only Studiematch's administrators can see user information. We never sell your information and don't share it with employers. When you apply for a job, you do it directly with the employer.

We use these providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare: runs the website and the database.
  • Resend: sends our emails, like job emails and password resets.
  • Anthropic: reads CVs and unknown degrees, and translates job descriptions, as described under AI.

All three are American companies, so data may be processed in the US. The transfer is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which they have joined.

How long

  • We keep your account, profile and CV as long as you have an account. If you delete it, we delete everything at once, including the list of job emails we have sent you.
  • What you do on the site is unlinked from your account after 6 months and deleted completely after 12 months.
  • Statistics for visitors without an account are deleted after 12 months.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile on the site, and you can delete your account under Profile → Your data. You also have the right to access your information, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, and object. Write to matchstudie@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.
